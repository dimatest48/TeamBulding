# User Personas — Student Task Tracker

> Based on: Brief.pdf  
> Audience: students of schools, colleges, and universities  
> Platform: Web (React + Python REST API)

---

## Persona 1 — Mykhailo, The Overwhelmed Freshman

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Age** | 18 |
| **Occupation** | First-year university student, Computer Science |
| **Income** | Low — stipend + parental support |
| **Tech literacy** | 4 / 5 |

**Pain points with similar products:**
- Uses Telegram group chats to track assignments — gets buried under memes and off-topic messages
- Juggles 7+ subjects simultaneously and loses the "big picture" of his total workload
- Procrastinates on low-priority tasks until they become critical the night before
- Tried Notion but abandoned it — too much setup and maintenance under exam stress

**Motivation & goal:**
Avoid failing courses because of forgotten deadlines. Wants a single, structured view of all tasks grouped by subject so he can decide what to work on each morning without opening five different apps.

**Quote:**
> *"I have five labs due this week and I only remembered about them today."*

## Persona 2 — Olena, The Diligent High-Schooler

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Age** | 16 |
| **Occupation** | 10th-grade student, preparing for university entrance exams |
| **Income** | None — fully dependent on parents |
| **Tech literacy** | 3 / 5 |

**Pain points with similar products:**
- Relies on a paper notebook she sometimes leaves at school
- Doesn't prioritize tasks well — spends equal time on every subject regardless of urgency
- Gets overwhelmed when tasks pile up with no visual signal of what is most urgent
- Wants to coordinate homework with classmates but currently does it through Viber and Instagram DMs

**Motivation & goal:**
Stay organized and share specific homework assignments with classmates without switching between four different apps. Needs a tool simple enough to use between classes in under a minute.

**Quote:**
> *"I write everything down but then I can't find the right page."*

## Persona 3 — Dmytro, The Group Project Leader

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Age** | 22 |
| **Occupation** | 4th-year Management student, part-time barista |
| **Income** | Medium — part-time wages + stipend |
| **Tech literacy** | 3 / 5 |

**Pain points with similar products:**
- Coordinates group projects across multiple courses via endless WhatsApp threads
- Needs to delegate tasks and see who completed what — currently has zero visibility
- Forgets which tasks he shared with which person after a few days
- Tried Jira and Trello — far too complex and time-consuming for a student group project

**Motivation & goal:**
Delegate tasks to teammates and track their progress without leaving the same tool he uses for his own personal assignments. Wants simplicity, not a project management suite.

**Quote:**
> *"Every group project turns into a mess because nobody knows who's doing what."*
